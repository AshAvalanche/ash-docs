# Connect to a dApp with WalletConnect

You can use a Safe account to connect to a dApp with **WalletConnect**.

On a compatible dApp, (e.g.: [Blackhole](https://www.blackhole.xyz/)), select WalletConnect as the wallet provider and click on the **Copy link** button:

![WalletConnect](/img/ash-wallet-walletconnect-1.png)

On Ash Wallet, click on the WalletConnect logo on the top right corner:

![WalletConnect](/img/ash-wallet-walletconnect-2.png)

You can then **paste** WalletConnect link:

![WalletConnect](/img/ash-wallet-walletconnect-3.png)

After pressing the **Approve** button, you can go back to the dApp and start using your Safe account to sign transactions. Trigger a transaction you should be prompted to sign it in Ash Wallet.
